AXIOMS

— WatchLLM Design Principle
01
No probabilistic guardrails. If your enforcement depends on an LLM saying "no", it can also say "yes". That's not safety — that's theatre.
02
Everything compiles offline. No cloud. No API. No vibes. The kernel works on an air-gapped laptop the same as a GitHub Actions runner.
03
Automation is the point. WatchLLM is a cron job for your codebase — continuously intercepting, continuously enforcing, never sleeping. Zero human in the enforcement path.
04
Suggestions don't stop $47k bots in Minsk. Deterministic rules do.
